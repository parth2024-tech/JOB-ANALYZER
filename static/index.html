Privacy Policy

Zero tracking • Local-first privacy architecture

1. Zero User Data Harvesting

CyberSec Intel Grid is an open engineering platform. We do not require user accounts, passwords, or personal profiles. We do not operate tracking beacons, third-party analytics scripts, marketing cookies, or tracking pixels.

2. Client-Side Resume & Preferences Storage

When you provide skills or parse a resume in the Matcher tool, all analysis occurs entirely in your browser memory and is persisted only in your local browser localStorage. Your resume text, skills, application state, and watchlists are never transmitted to or stored on our servers.

3. Public Job Telemetry

All job postings and corporate metadata aggregated by this platform are retrieved from publicly accessible corporate career portals (Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, Ashby) and public developer APIs. We index publicly available metadata to assist early-career cybersecurity professionals in discovering legitimate career opportunities.

4. Open Source & Transparency

The entire pipeline codebase is publicly auditable. No telemetry is monetized or shared with third parties.

Terms of Service

Fair use career research platform

1. Purpose of Service

CyberSec Intel Grid is provided free of charge for informational, educational, and job search purposes. It aggregates publicly available job postings from official corporate career websites.

While our automated verification bots continuously validate URL status codes and expiration indicators, job openings can close or change without warning. Users are responsible for verifying job details directly on the hiring employer's official career portal before applying.
